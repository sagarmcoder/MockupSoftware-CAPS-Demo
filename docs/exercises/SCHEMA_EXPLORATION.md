# Level 7 — Database Exploration

Do not start by guessing a query. Inspect tables, keys, and sample rows first.

1. Management wants total approved billable hours per client for August 2026.
2. Which employees logged time against inactive or cancelled projects?
3. Which clients had activity during the pay period before the latest one?
4. Which employees worked overtime but do not have an approved timesheet for that period?
5. Which active clients have no active projects?
6. Which projects exceed their hour budget, and by how much?
7. Which departments have employees assigned to projects but no approved entries in August?
8. Find incomplete entries and enough related information for a manager to follow up.
9. Identify employees whose latest recorded activity is older than 60 days relative to September 21, 2026. Keep employees with no activity.
10. Produce a compact payroll-review dataset for one period: employee, department, timesheet status, regular hours, overtime hours, and incomplete-entry count.

For Exercises 1 and 10, explain the intended output grain before writing SQL.
