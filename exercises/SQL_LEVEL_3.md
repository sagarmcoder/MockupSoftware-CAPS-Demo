# SQL Level 3 — Joins

Do not assume every prompt needs the same join type.

1. Show each employee with department and role names.
2. Show every project with its client name.
3. Show time entries with employee, project, and client names.
4. Show every employee and total approved hours, including employees with no entries.
5. Show every client and its project count, including clients with no projects.
6. Find projects that have no time entries.
7. Show each timesheet with employee name and pay-period dates.
8. List work items and the assigned employee name; keep unassigned work items.
9. Show employees assigned to more than one active project.
10. Find employees whose department differs from their manager's department.

For Exercises 4 and 5, predict what an `INNER JOIN` would remove before running anything.
